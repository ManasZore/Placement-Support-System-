# Placement Operations Copilot 🚀

A web-based placement management platform designed to help students evaluate their technical skills, practice mock interviews, and apply for relevant job roles. The platform also empowers placement administrators and companies to post job openings and manage candidate applications efficiently.

---

## 📌 Project Overview
Many students face challenges during campus recruitment due to a lack of clear understanding regarding placement readiness, role suitability, and interview practice. Simultaneously, placement officers and recruiters struggle with manual tracking and fragmented student data[cite: 1]. 

**Placement Operations Copilot** addresses these challenges by consolidating skill evaluation, role recommendation, practice mock interviews, and job application management into a centralized web platform.

---

## ✨ Key Features

* **Skill Evaluation Module**: Evaluates students across core technical domains (e.g., Python, SQL, Excel, ML, Statistics) and calculates placement readiness scores.
* **Role Recommendation Engine**: Matches student skill profiles against real-world job role requirements to highlight suitable career paths[cite: 2].
* **Interactive Mock Interviews**: Offers practice interview sessions tailored to specific job roles and difficulty levels[cite: 2].
* **Centralized Job Portal**: Allows companies/admins to post job vacancies, set eligibility criteria, and review candidate applications.
* **Student Dashboard**: Tracks job application histories, interview feedback, and performance progress over time[cite: 2].
